Hello everybody! We're releasing **Update 1.20.1** today to tackle issues and balance concerns reported by players since the release of _By God Alone_ and the 1.20 "Crozier" Update. We'll continue addressing your feedback and bug reports, but given the relatively short time until the release of _Silk & Silver_, we will be targeting its accompanying 1.21 Update release for any changes going forward.


Check the changelog below for more information!

# Update 1.20.1 Changelog


## Game Content

-   Added historical sainthood and burial sites for Clotilde, Sigismund, Guntram, Sigebert III, and William of Gellone.
-   The Monk trait is now selectable in the Ruler Designer.
-   Custom rulers can now be created for landless Archbishops and Cardinals.
-   Updated the Confucian saint designation from Shengxian to Xianru.
-   Added historical glossary to some tenets, separating their historical background links from their descriptions and names.
-   Added auto-fill buttons when editing Rite names.


## Game Balance

-   Ask Subordinates for Gold now costs between 100 and 250 Piety, scaling with the amount of Gold requested.
-   Spiritual Fulfillment from Cranial Trophies now scales with the victim’s Prestige.
-   Religious Scholar's lifestyle XP bonus is halved, from up to +50% to up to +25%.
-   Passive rite learning while travelling has a 90-day cooldown instead of 30.
-   Beseech De Jure Rights has a 3-year cooldown for the asker instead of a per-cleric one.
-   Characters who gain raiding through a personal tenet can no longer use the default Pillage intent.
-   Request White Peace now requires the war to have lasted at least two years.
-   Ecumenical Council debates now have an AI opponent who makes their own sway attempts and cannot be swayed, unless a player already leads the opposing side.
-   Negotiate for Land can now request a primary title, with harder acceptance and only the small refusal penalty for its holder.
-   Heads of Faith on the mainline rite can still use Sway to My Rite to call Heads of Rite back to it, at a substantial acceptance penalty.
-   Befriending a Head of Faith at a feast now retains the Befriend scheme until friendship is established and shows potential friendship in their event.
-   Added a recipient cooldown to Bestow De Jure Lands.
-   Heads of Faith now take two Feasts to befriend, and clergy claim and land negotiations are harder to chain.
-   Removed the high minimum chance for AI rulers to accept petitions.
-   Reduced Study Rite success bonuses: Learning now contributes ×2 instead of ×3, each Piety level contributes +3 instead of +5, and Theologian contributes +10 instead of +20.
-   Ecumenical Council sways now scale with the size of the proposed change, up to 2.5 times the base cost. Free trait-based sways affect fewer attendees for major changes, and duels over radical proposals are slightly harder.
-   Independent kings and emperors of at least Paragon of Virtue Piety can now create Holy Sites in their realm when the county follows their faith and their Head of Faith has a positive opinion of them.
-   Rebalanced Assume the Conqueror’s Mantle around the Become Conqueror requirements and set its Piety cost to 3,000.
-   Raised Canonize a Saint’s base costs from 2,000 to 3,000 Piety and from 300 to 500 Gold, and added an opinion requirement when Head of Faith approval is needed.
-   Editing the mainline rite now requires proposed tenets to be at least Very Popular.
-   Grand Cathedral bell contributions no longer require a city or temple holding in the contributor’s domain.


## AI

-   The AI no longer declares war on the Frankokratia leader while the Fourth Crusade story is running.
-   The AI now respects its relations with a banner’s dynasty when deciding whether to gift it.
-   The AI now only revokes titles for a rival or wrong-faith vassal when doing so would not incur Tyranny.
-   Nomad invasion AI avoids island kingdom targets again.
-   The AI now respects Influence and Honor limits when harming title candidacies.
-   AI vassals can now use Claim Throne when enabled by a Struggle, even if they are not powerful vassals.
-   AI diarchs now recruit heads of powerful families for coups, allowing coups to work in realms without powerful vassals.
-   A new AI Pope no longer recasts a core tenet of a rite a player edited.
-   AI attendees can no longer sway back Ecumenical Council attendees who have already been swayed during the current debate.
-   The AI now avoids demanding children for the cloister who are married, have children, or are heirs, and remembers refusals.
-   AI clerics no longer adopt other rulers’ heirs or player courtiers as theocratic heirs.
-   The AI Pope no longer appoints a player’s unlanded courtiers as Cardinals.
-   The AI no longer demands vows of chastity from chaste characters.
-   AI Ecumenical Council hosts now generally propose the nearest doctrine change, with exceptions for Heralds, Heresiarchs, madness, or strong preferences.
-   Emperors can restore Backwater counties, and the AI now takes the decision.
-   The AI no longer overrides its restriction against choosing Eastern-rite Cardinals before the Great Schism.
-   Before the Great Schism the Patriarch of Constantinople cannot become a cardinal, and the AI only picks Western-rite cardinals.
-   AI clergy invitations to Ecumenical Councils now follow clerical gender doctrine rather than ruling gender, allowing councils to reach quorum.
-   Chaplain auto-fill prefers the owner's rite, and AI heads of faith only seat archbishops of their own rite.
-   AI rulers of Meritocratic Khanates no longer indiscriminately raze holdings.
-   New AI Popes, including Populist candidates, only make vice or abomination tenets core when an Ecumenical Council would allow it.
-   The AI no longer spends a player lessee’s Treasury on building construction.
