# Update 1.20.1.2 Changelog


## Bugfixes

-   Fixed a bug for resizing text in College of Cardinals that could appear in languages other than English
-   Fixed a bug where the revoke interaction could break the UI in languages other than English
-   Fixed a bug where Russian rites and faith names would use cut-off adjectives
-   Fixed a number of crashes that could occur when rendering portraits
-   Fixed a bug where monasteries could not be bestowed to monastic holy orders
-   Fixed a bug where the create holy site button was hidden without By God Alone despite meeting non-DLC related requirements
-   Fixed a bug where dying as an adventurer after procuring an estate left you landless and would trigger a game over
-   Religious Artifact inspirations no longer show up without By God Alone, as they could not be sponsored without it
